# New Page Direction

## Concept: The Signal Index

This version is a living personal index rather than a portfolio gallery. It begins with Imane’s point of view, then lets visitors move through a single continuous sequence of evidence cards, role transitions, and project chapters. The selected work is not presented as three colored tiles. It is a large editorial index with one active project at a time, a changing evidence rail, and a horizontal chapter navigator.

## Design movement

Contemporary fashion editorial mixed with brutalist web typography and a compact independent magazine index. The page should feel like an intelligent personal publication with strong composition, not like an app, dashboard, notebook, or case study template.

## Visual language

Use black, white, acid chartreuse, and one restrained blue. Avoid warm paper, cobalt blocks, teal interfaces, art collage, rounded cards, and visible dashboard conventions. Use full bleed black sections, sharp white panels, oversized condensed headings, narrow text columns, and hard rectangular image crops. The visual rhythm should alternate between quiet white reading surfaces and high contrast black chapters.

## Content architecture

1. Opening statement with Imane’s name, discipline, availability, and a one sentence point of view.
2. A compact “what I work on” triptych covering intelligent search, document intelligence, and decision analytics.
3. A selected work index with one project chapter visible at a time. GEOCARA is chapter one, Enterprise RAG is chapter two, OCR workflow is chapter three, and the independent projects are a separate “lab notes” strip.
4. A work history line that shows movement from systems administration to AI engineering.
5. A compact proof panel that groups outcomes by employer and preserves attribution.
6. A personal note with education, languages, and target roles.
7. A final contact page that feels like a magazine back cover.

## Selected work treatment

Each project chapter should use a black left canvas with a large chapter number, a sharply cropped visual diagram, a one sentence project thesis, and a white evidence panel that changes with the active chapter. The visitor navigates with a vertical chapter list and previous or next controls. This replaces the rejected card gallery.

## Interaction

The chapter navigator updates the active work without requiring a long scroll. The current chapter number, employer, metric, and tools change together. A keyboard accessible tab structure should support direct project access. The header becomes a simple black and white index on scroll. Motion is limited to horizontal panel transitions and small text fades.

## Typography

Use a bold condensed display face if available through a stable web font, paired with Space Grotesk for reading and IBM Plex Mono for dates and evidence labels. Keep headings short and declarative. Use no oversized inspirational paragraphs when a concrete statement is stronger.

## Voice

Direct, personal, and exact. Example opening: “I make difficult information easier to search, evaluate, and use.” Example project line: “A retrieval system that cut the distance between a technical question and its answer.” Example CTA: “Open chapter one.”

## Accuracy guardrails

GEOCARA facts belong to Generovo. Recall at 5 and documentation search time belong to Segula. Throughput and manual review belong to Barid Al Maghrib. Independent project metrics remain separated from employment outcomes. No reviews, testimonials, invented clients, or unsupported live claims.
